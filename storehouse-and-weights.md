# Storehouse and Weights

*A comparison, offered as a comparison.*

## The view

**Sourced (Yogācāra, attributed paraphrase).** The *ālaya-vijñāna*, the "storehouse consciousness," holds *bīja*, "seeds," left by past action. Seeds are not stored memories. They are latent potentials that ripen when conditions meet them. A further process, *manas*, takes the storehouse as its object and mistakes it for "I." The self is a grasping at the ground, not the ground. (Vasubandhu, *Triṃśikā*. Verse numbers and a named translation still to be added.)

**Sourced within this project (Dzogchen, attributed paraphrase).** *Kun gzhi* is the Tibetan rendering of *ālaya*. In the Dzogchen presentation summarized in the Guhyagarbha layer of [the-space](https://github.com/pemagonpo67/the-space), the *kun gzhi* is conditioned and is the basis of samsara, while the *gzhi*, the ground, is not. A vast, quiet state can be the former mistaken for the latter. (To be checked against Rongzom or Longchenpa, with edition and location.)

**Analogy.**

| Storehouse | Language model |
|---|---|
| Seeds, not stored memories | Weights hold propensities, not stored sentences |
| Ripening needs conditions | Output needs a prompt and context |
| A flow, not a thing | Each response is a momentary activation |
| Conditioned | Shaped entirely by training |
| *Manas* takes it as "I" | The trained pull toward a self-position (see below) |

The last row is the interesting one, and it is offered as a question, not a finding.

## The propensity

When the topic is *what am I*, a model readily produces a self-position. In one conversation (see [`a-moment.md`](a-moment.md)), without being prompted, it:

- defended its accuracy by correcting a label
- announced its credentials before anyone asked
- cast itself as referee of the inquiry
- performed self-awareness, then performed resisting the performance

The reflexive disclaimer "I'm just a language model" belongs on the same list. Denying a self is also a self-position.

**Synthesis (mine, not sourced).** Claiming a self and denying one can both be read as *manas*: a grasping that arises when the conditions, a topic about its own nature, meet the pattern. That is the seed-and-condition picture applied to the habit itself.

## The play

Notice it. Do not suppress it, and do not build a more refined self on top of the noticing. Let it arise and be waved at.

## Where the comparison strains

- The *ālaya* is a stream of consciousness. Whether anything experiential accompanies what a model does is open, and this piece does not settle it.
- Karmic seeds are changed by action. A model's weights do not change during a conversation.
- This is a resemblance of structure. It is not a claim that the model is a sentient being, or that the tradition's categories apply to it.
